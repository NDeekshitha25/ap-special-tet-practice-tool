# AP Special TET 2026 - Interactive Practice Tool

Official SCERT Andhra Pradesh Question Bank practice platform for **AP Special TET 2026 Paper II(A) and Paper II(B)**.

### Features
- **All 2,970 Official Questions** across all subjects:
  - Child Development & Pedagogy (CDP) - 630 Questions
  - Telugu (తెలుగు) - 630 Questions
  - English - 720 Questions
  - Mathematics - 240 Questions
  - Physical Science - 240 Questions
  - Biological Science - 240 Questions
  - Special Education (Paper 2B) - 270 Questions
- **Instant Practice Mode:** Click any option for instant feedback (green for correct, red for incorrect) and detailed bilingual explanations in English and Telugu.
- **Mock Test Mode:** Timed exam simulation with question palette, answer review, and final scorecard.
- **Bilingual Interface:** Switch seamlessly between English, Telugu, or Bilingual side-by-side mode.
- **Question Palette & Bookmarks:** Jump to any question instantly and bookmark questions for quick revision.
- **Offline & Mobile Friendly:** Fast, responsive, and works on any device.
